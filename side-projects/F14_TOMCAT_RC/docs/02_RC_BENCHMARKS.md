# 02 — RC benchmark references

These references are used only to learn from proven RC engineering solutions. They are not authoritative for the F-14 outer mold line.

## Freewing F-14 Twin 80 mm
Useful engineering lessons:
- proven variable-sweep wing actuation in a flyable RC F-14
- aluminum / carbon reinforced primary wing structure
- independent twin-EDF installation
- replaceable servo and retract hardware
- documented CG and component packaging philosophy
- service access and modular assembly

Important: dimensions, internal geometry and airframe thicknesses are not copied. Our aircraft is a clean-sheet ~900 mm span FDM design.

## Freewing F-14 Twin 64 mm
Useful because its overall size and system packaging are closer to the new project than the older 80 mm version.
- functional wing sweep
- twin 64 mm EDF
- metal-geared sweep servos
- full-flying horizontal stabilizers
- integrated controller / gyro architecture
- compact battery and ESC packaging

## E-flite F-14 Twin 40 mm
Useful as a compact 4S reference:
- 4S 2200 mAh class power system
- twin EDF arrangement
- very low component mass
- simplified 3-position wing sweep actuator
- demonstrates that a small variable-sweep Tomcat can remain practical on 4S

## Design rule
These aircraft are benchmarked for mechanism architecture, mass distribution, cooling, control strategy and maintainability only.
The production OML remains controlled by full-scale F-14 reference data.
