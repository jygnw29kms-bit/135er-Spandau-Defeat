# Rathaus Spandau - Architecture Production Reference

Updated: 2026-10-14

Purpose: hard geometry targets for the 135er Spandau Strike Blender world master. Reference/photogrammetry meshes are measurement aids only and must not become the visible final game building.

## Verified architectural targets

- construction period: 1910-1913
- architects: Heinrich Reinhardt and Georg Suessenguth
- style: Reformstil with partly baroque facade forms
- main facade length: 116 m
- tower height: 80 m
- rectangular building with three symmetric courtyards
- high mansard roof
- base level plus three upper storeys and a mezzanine termination
- seven-part articulation of the facade/window axes
- central gabled risalit
- tower carries four clock faces
- present tower top is the post-war 1957 simplified/reworked termination; the two small towers formerly flanking the central risalit are no longer present

## Modelling consequences

1. Preserve the measured 116 m facade and 80 m tower as hard QA dimensions.
2. Build the three-courtyard massing and roof silhouette before micro-detail.
3. Central risalit, main portals, mansard/dormer rhythm and clock-stage silhouette are Tier-1 recognition features.
4. Do not reintroduce the two historical risalit turrets into the modern map.
5. Decorative repetition should use reusable/instanced modules; unique geometry is reserved for silhouette and landmark features.
6. Berlin3D/LoD2/DGM/OSM remain spatial evidence, not final visible architecture.
