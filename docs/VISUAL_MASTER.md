# Visual Master Standard — 135er Spandau Defeat

Status: **binding art direction** · active engine: **Source 2 / CS2**.

The approved concept/map-preview images are optical-master references for final graphical output. They define composition, density, lighting, readability, first-person framing, objective language and environmental identity.

They are **not** presented as Source 2 screenshots.

## Source 2 rendering target

Production work should reproduce the optical master through the actual Source 2 / CS2 toolchain:
- Source 2 PBR material workflow
- Hammer-authored geometry and props
- physically believable direct/indirect lighting
- Source 2 fog/particles where appropriate
- strong material readability at FPS viewing distance
- balanced visibility before cinematic effects
- restrained post-processing
- high-resolution textures where justified
- stable multiplayer performance on the CS2 runtime

## Camera / presentation

- 16:9 master composition
- natural first-person eye height
- weapon framing must not obscure objective routes
- restrained field of view
- teammates should read clearly against environment
- landmark recognition takes priority over spectacle

## HUD language

The optical master uses blue/red team state, match clock, A/B/C objectives and compact tactical information. Any public HUD shown as **in-game** must be produced by the actual Source 2 / CS2 runtime.

## Image-role rule

**Optical master / concept:** may be generated or externally rendered, but must be labeled as concept/reference.

**In-game:** must be captured directly from the running Source 2 / CS2 project.

Historical art may be period-authentic, but fictional combat must not be described as documented history.
