# Visual Master Standard — Source 2

Status: **binding art direction**.

Approved generated previews define composition, density, lighting, first-person framing and overall mood. They are concept targets only. A final image may be labeled an in-engine screenshot only after it is captured from the actual CS2 / Source 2 map.

## Source 2 production target
- CS2 Workshop Tools / Hammer
- Source 2 PBR materials
- baked/dynamic lighting appropriate to CS2 map production
- high-detail environment meshes with sensible gameplay collision
- 16:9 first-person composition
- competitive readability at gameplay distance

## Authenticity rule
Historical art may be period-authentic, but fictional combat must not be described as documented history.
