# Historical map set — 135er Spandau Defeat

## Authenticity rule

The project separates **documented history** from **gameplay fiction**. A real historical site may be used as a battlefield or objective space only when the game clearly states whether combat at that exact location is documented or whether the scenario is historically inspired.

| Map ID | Period | Historical basis | Gameplay treatment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `fort_hahneberg_1945` | 1945 | Fort Hahneberg, Spandau/Staaken | historically inspired fortress scenario; not claimed as a documented battle inside the fort |
| `teufelsberg_coldwar` | ca. 1985 | US listening station; Berlin special map outside Spandau | Cold-War alternate scenario; no claim of a historical firefight |
| `flugplatz_gatow_1945` | 1945 | military airfield built in the 1930s and occupied at the end of WWII | combat-era airfield setting with runway, hangars and academy compounds |
| `gatow_luftbruecke_1948` | 1948/49 | RAF Gatow and the Berlin Airlift | logistics/objective map; not represented as a historical ground battle |
| `radeland_1945` | 1945 | Radelandstraße complex used by Soviet and then British forces | occupation-period barracks scenario |
| `hakenfelde_heeresamt_1944` | 1944 | wartime armaments/industrial facilities near the Havel | industrial/warehouse objective map; not a claim of one specific battle |
| `zitadelle_1945` | 1 May 1945 | the Citadel surrendered without a fight | approaches/security scenario; never marketed as “Battle of the Citadel” |
| `britischer_sektor_spandau` | Cold War | extensive British military presence in Spandau | barracks/checkpoint training or alternate-conflict scenario |

## Environment-art direction

Historical maps use period-appropriate architecture, signage, road surfaces, vehicles, lighting fixtures, uniforms and props. Totalitarian symbols, where legally permitted and historically necessary, are contextual historical material only and are never celebratory or propagandistic.

The final production target is photorealistic Unreal Engine 5.8 environment art using original/licensed assets, documented reference material, Nanite/PBR materials, Lumen, Virtual Shadow Maps, World Partition/HLOD and authored collision. The current generated grayboxes are gameplay scaffolding only.
