# Project Vision

## Identity

135er – Spandau Defeat is not a generic WWII shooter. Its core identity is **Spandau across time**.

Players should recognize the district through landmarks, architecture, road patterns, residential areas, water, bridges, industrial sites, parks, school campuses and historic structures.

## Engine direction

The active engine/runtime direction is **Source 2 through Counter-Strike 2**.

The previous Unreal Engine direction is retired and must not be used for new production work.

Authoring is based around:
- Hammer
- CS2 Workshop Tools
- Source 2 materials and lighting
- CS2-compatible gameplay/map scripting
- Linux dedicated-server deployment

## Gameplay baseline

The design target is the direct multiplayer feel associated with classic Day of Defeat:
- fast spawn-to-action loop
- low time-to-kill
- distinct weapon roles
- meaningful class choice
- crouch/standing combat with appropriate movement restrictions
- deployable/support weapon concepts where technically supported
- grenades and suppression-like pressure
- objective-driven rounds
- minimal progression friction
- community-server friendly design

## Era model

Maps may represent pre-1945, WWII/1945-inspired, post-war, Berlin Airlift, late Cold War and modern Spandau. A real place may have multiple era variants with changed cover, routes, vehicles and atmosphere.

## Visual target

Approved generated previews remain the binding **optical master**:
- photorealistic environment target
- realistic materials and believable lighting
- strong landmark readability
- restrained cinematic smoke/particles
- clear silhouettes and objective lanes
- minimal tactical HUD language
- no browser-game presentation
- real-place fidelity before spectacle

These previews are not gameplay screenshots. Any image labeled **in-game** must be captured from the running Source 2 / CS2 project.
